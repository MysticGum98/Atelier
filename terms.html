Terms

Last updated 10 October 2026. Short, because the app is simple.

What Zay is

Zay is a personal wardrobe app. It keeps the clothes you add, puts outfits together from them by colour, proportion and occasion, and can ask an AI stylist to read photos and answer questions. It is made and run by its author in Amman, Jordan.

Your account

  • You need to be 16 or older to create an account.
  • You sign in with a code sent to your email. Keep that inbox yours; anyone who can read it can sign in as you.
  • One account is for one person. A linked partner has their own account.

Your content

  • Your photos, pieces, notes and looks are yours. Zay gets only the permission it needs to store them, show them back to you, and send them to the stylist when you ask.
  • Only upload photos you have the right to use. If a photo shows another person, make sure they are fine with it.
  • You can export everything as a backup at any time, and delete everything with one button in Settings.

The stylist

  • Scores, suggestions and answers are opinions from rules and from an AI model. They are there to help you decide, not to decide for you, and they can be wrong.
  • Reading photos and asking the stylist use a shared service with a daily allowance per person: at the moment 120 photo readings, 60 written answers and 10 searches a day. The numbers can change; the app shows what is used and when the count starts again.
  • Do not use the stylist to generate content that is unlawful, harmful or about a person who has not agreed to it.

Availability and changes

  • Zay is offered as it is, free of charge for now, without a promise that it is always available or free of mistakes. Keep a backup of anything you would mind losing.
  • Features, limits and these terms can change. When something that matters changes, the date at the top moves and the app says so once.
  • Accounts used to abuse the service or other people can be closed.
