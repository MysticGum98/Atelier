The sign-in code did not arrive

Codes come from noreply@zaystylist.com within a minute. Check the Junk or Promotions folder once; if it is there, mark it as not junk and the next one lands in your inbox. A code works for ten minutes and once. If nothing arrives, ask for a new one, and if that fails, write to us with the address you used.

My clothes are not showing after I changed phone

Sign in on the new phone with the same email. Your closet, photos, looks and wears come down from your account on their own. If you never signed in on the old phone, the closet lived only there: open the old phone, go to Settings, and use Backup to save a file, then Restore it on the new one.

A photo cut out badly

Zay cuts the garment out of the photo so it sits on the page. It works best with the piece laid flat or hung alone against a plain wall in daylight. Open the piece, choose Edit, and retake the photo; the old cut-out is replaced.

A score feels wrong

Every score shows its reasons. Open the look and read them; if a reason is wrong for you, reject the look and Zay stops suggesting that pairing. If a rule itself seems wrong, tell us which look and why. The rules are written down and they change when someone argues well.

Subscriptions, trials and refunds

Zay the Stylist renews on its own until you cancel. Cancel any time in your phone's Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions; you keep the stylist until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple: open reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase. If you reinstall the app or change phones, use Restore purchases in Settings.

Delete my account

Settings, then Delete account. It removes your account, your closet, your photos and your looks from our servers at once, and it cannot be undone. Save a backup first if you want to keep anything.

What Zay keeps about me

Only what it needs to dress you, and nothing for advertising. The privacy page lists it all in plain words. The terms are short and also in plain words.
